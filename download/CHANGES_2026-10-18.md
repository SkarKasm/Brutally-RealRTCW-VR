# Brutally RealRTCW VR: October 18 update

A full mod release for single player, made from the GitHub source (main, commit 57f9dbb) with the engine and renderer that match it. Unzip it into your RealRTCW folder. It holds only the mod's own files, the `game` folder and the launchers, and never touches RealRTCW's `Main` folder.

## Survival
- **Waves keep going past wave 1.** Before, an enemy who'd been shot down, lost an arm or surrendered came back in the next wave unarmed and frozen in place, and stalled the waves. They now come back armed and fighting.
- **Killing an enemy who surrendered no longer breaks the waves.**
- **No more free weapons on the floor.** Enemies who drop their gun (shot down, crippled or surrendering) no longer leave a weapon you can pick up.
- **New HUD:** ENEMIES LEFT shows at the top of the screen. When 3 or fewer are left, a small compass points at each one and shows how far away it is.
- **Fixed** a rare crash when an enemy died with no attacker.

## Gore
- **Limbs come off properly.** Before, a second copy of the whole body went flying. The last full package had shipped an older renderer that couldn't cut a limb off a body.
- **Every character can lose limbs, elite guards included.** The limb really comes off now, so you no longer get a stump drawn over an arm or leg that's still attached.
- **Smaller bullet wounds.** The holes keep the same look but are about the size of the round, and they sit where the shot went in.

## Enemy Territory SP
- **Starts and loads levels again.** The menu memory was too small for ET's menus, which made the game crash on startup.

## Tested
Tested on a monitor: Survival waves 1 to 9, the HUD counter and compass, decapitations, throat and belly kills, limbs coming off soldiers, trench soldiers and elite guards, picking up bodies without a headset, ET's menu and a level briefing, a campaign level, and Cursed Sands' menu and Survival.

Not tested in VR.
