# October 10 update

Thanks for all the feedback on the last update. This one is all about how your arms and guns feel in VR.

**Arms**
- Your HD arms now have a real wrist and elbow that bend as you move the controllers, and they join BJ's shoulders, so you see one whole arm instead of a stiff one. The shoulders are spaced out to where they really are.
- Both hands match on every gun. Where a gun came with a smaller hand, only that hand's palm and fingers stretch to match the other one, so your arms stay the same, and the right hand no longer shrinks on pistols.
- A magazine in your hand now turns with your hand instead of pointing the way the gun does, and lines up with the gun as you bring it to the well.

**Rifles**
- The Kar98, Mosin, G43, Garand, MP44, Sten, MP34, Ithaca and more now have a right hand on the wrist of the stock, where a real hand grips it, instead of clipping through the gun.
- Bolt actions stay shut after a shot until you work the bolt yourself.
- Working the bolt like the real thing: hold the rifle with both hands and tap the right grip. Your right hand lets go and your left hand keeps the rifle up. Grab the bolt with the right trigger and pull it back, then grip the rifle again to take it back.
- The left hand now grabs where BJ holds each gun in the flatscreen game, like the MP40's magazine well, with a bigger zone. The bolt and slide zones are smaller so the two don't clash.

**Weapon wheel**
- The wheel is smaller and split into pages of six, so the icons no longer crowd each other. Flick the right stick left or right while it's open to turn the page.
- The last page has the binoculars (once you've found them), Quick Save and Quick Load, each with its own icon.
- The night vision rifle is now called the Snooper Rifle in the wheel, instead of Garand.

**Binoculars**
- Once you've found the binoculars they're in the weapon wheel. Pick them and they're in your right hand: raise them to your eyes to look through them. Pick any weapon to put them away.
- Raising your empty left hand to your eyes no longer brings up the binoculars.

**Guns**
- The Venom, flamethrower, Tesla gun and M2 Browning are held by your right hand on their grip. Before, the right controller showed the left hand the flat game draws on them.
- Hands and guns are drawn 1.15x bigger, since RTCW's guns and hands came out small next to the world.
- The PPSh and the Snooper Rifle are bigger, closer to their real size, and your right hand holds them by the stock.
- The Venom and the Tesla gun are much bigger, as the heavy weapons they are. Your hands on them are the same size as on every other gun.
- Your right hand sits on the real pistol grip of the Venom, the Tesla gun and the hand-held MG42.
- Pistol slides, and the bolts of the SMGs and semi-auto rifles, cycle with each shot. Pistol slides, and the bolts of the M1 Garand, G43 and M1941, lock back when the gun is empty.
- The revolver's cylinder swings out when it's empty.
- With hand reloading, bolt-action rifles and the pump shotgun no longer throw out the spent case when they fire. It comes out when you work the bolt or the pump.
- Mounted MG42s and flak guns work in VR: aim them with your gun hand and pull the trigger. The gun stops at its limits instead of spinning your whole view around, and you can mount one while bending down to it.

**Dual wielding fixes**
- Each gun is held by a real gripping hand: the left hand is a mirrored copy of the right one, and the left gun sits in that palm, so no more guns clipping through your palms.
- The left gun has its own aiming dot.
- Each gun has its own magazine.
- X drops the left gun's magazine and A the right one's.
- Reloading at the hip works: hold a hand down at your hip to reload that gun.

**Aiming**
- The MP40 and MP44 now fire from their muzzles, so the dot (where your bullets go) lines up with the barrel. Before, their shots came from behind your hand.
- Every gun's iron sights now line up with where it shoots: look down the sights and the dot sits on the front sight. Before, most guns were tilted a few degrees off, and the two pistols when dual wielding pointed away from each other.

**Throwing**
- Grenades, dynamite and thrown knives now land where the dot points: aim, hold the trigger, let go. No more grenades dropping at your feet. Prefer real throws? VR OPTIONS, "Grenades, knife throws: By hand".

**Leaning and crouching**
- Your head can now lean out up to 40 cm past your body before it pulls the body along, so you can crouch over a hatch, go prone or reach over a fireplace without being pushed back. You still can't put your head through walls. (VR OPTIONS, "Lean out over body".)

**Controls**
- The right stick no longer switches weapons. Up jumps and down crouches. Guns come from the weapon wheel on the right grip.
- Alt fire is off, since the scopes already work.

**Comfort**
- The 3D sky now sits at infinity instead of looking like a miniature in front of your face, so it no longer strains your eyes.
- New Height offset option in VR OPTIONS raises or lowers you by up to 30 cm, standing or seated.

**Menus**
- VR OPTIONS and GORE & EXTRAS are now in the pause menu too, so you can change them without leaving your game.

**Cursed Sands**
- New launchers, Cursed Sands SP VR.bat and Cursed Sands SP (no VR).bat, play RealRTCW's Cursed Sands campaign with everything above, VR menus included.

**Recording**
- The game window on your monitor now shows what you see in the headset, filling the whole window with no black bars, so you can record it.

**Install / update**
Extract the zip into your RealRTCW folder (normally ...\steamapps\common\RealRTCW) and overwrite when asked. Your settings and saves are kept.
