# Brutally RealRTCW VR: November 16 update

The November 14 update (full changelog below) plus two new ways to line the hands up on the guns.

## Hand alignment in VR (new)
Type `handtweak` in the console and line up the whole gun, the trigger hand and the support hand on each weapon while holding it in the headset: hold the off hand grip and move it, tap the off hand trigger for the next part. Each weapon remembers its own settings in `vr_handalign.txt`. See [HAND-ALIGN.md](../docs/HAND-ALIGN.md) for all the commands.

## Hand Editor (new)
A program on your PC for posing the hands and fingers on each VR weapon in 3D, finger by finger. Double-click `Start Hand Editor.cmd` in your RealRTCW folder and it opens in your browser (needs Python 3). Save, build and install your poses from the editor while the game is closed; RealRTCW's own files are never changed. See [the editor's README](../misc/vr/hand-editor/README.md).

# November 14 update, full changelog

Everything added since the last release (November 2). Most of it is single player and needs the Jolt
ragdolls (`vr_physics 1`, the default). Co-op is unchanged.

## Physical reactions (new)
Enemies no longer play canned hit and death animations. Their bodies are driven by muscles
and balance on a physics skeleton, and react to where and how hard they are hit.

- **Hit but alive:** the bullet shoves the body where it lands and that part flinches. He
  staggers a step or two, steps to catch himself, throws his arms out for balance (no
  windmilling), digs in, and eases back into his animation once he is steady. Arm hits and
  non-lethal torso hits no longer floor him.
- **Bosses fight on:** the Uebersoldaten and the crypt monster (Olaric) get no physics at
  all: no stagger, knockdown, disarm or bleeding while alive, and they die with their own
  death animations instead of a ragdoll. Heinrich doesn't stagger, get knocked down, drop
  his weapon or bleed out while he's alive; the physics takes him only once he dies.
- **Shooting back:** a man shot in the chest keeps his gun up while he staggers, tracks you
  with it and keeps firing. Each shot he fires kicks his gun arm back before he brings it
  back on you.
- **Clutching:** a man hit in the upper body keeps a hand on the wound while the other arm
  flails for balance. A chest hit brings both hands to it if he has no gun. While he staggers, his head turns
  toward where he was hit.
- **Knockdowns:** leg hits, heavy rounds and shots that leave him badly hurt can knock him
  down. He goes over when he is driven past about 55 degrees and can't right himself,
  toppling with his arms out, and breaks the fall with his arms.
- **Getting up:** once he has come to rest he gets up with a real get-up animation (off his
  back: up on his elbows, sits up, rolls onto a knee and stands; off his front: hands and
  knees, then up), laid where he lies and facing the way he lay. Shot or knocked down again on
  the way up, the physics takes over from the pose he is in.
- **Dying on his feet:** a lethal body shot is no longer an instant ragdoll. He stays up for
  about 1 to 3 seconds by wound (gut longest, leg shortest), doubled over or clutching it,
  then falls. Headshots and blasts still drop him limp at once.
- **On the floor:** the dying, the crippled and those bleeding out writhe for 5 to 10
  seconds: gasping, back arching, knees drawn up, one hand on the wound, the other clawing at
  the floor, fading until still.
- **Bleeding out:** a wound that doesn't kill keeps bleeding by where it hit (throat and
  upper thigh fastest, gut slowest; arms and lower legs bleed lightly and stop). Wounds add
  up. At 10% health he collapses to bleed out on the floor. Bosses don't bleed out.
- **Walk through them:** enemies who are staggering, down but alive, or getting up no longer
  block your way. Shots hit the body as you see it, and a staggering enemy recovers where the
  stagger took him instead of snapping back.
- **Dropped weapons:** shot in the right arm, he drops his weapon. Once he has recovered he
  crouches and has it again.
- **Grabbing** a living body makes it go limp in your hand.

## Headshots and damage
- Any headshot from the player kills outright, with any gun at any range (shotguns and long
  shots included). The line is drawn through the bullet's path about halfway down the face, so
  a shot into the eyes from below counts. Bosses, lopers and dogs are excluded. Kicks, knives
  and explosions to the head work as before.
- Enemies hurt each other in every mode: crossfire between AI now counts, at full damage.
  Lopers still don't hurt other lopers.
- A bullet pushes a living enemy about a third as hard as before. The killing shot is
  unchanged.

## Blood
- **Blood in the air** is drawn as droplets and beaded, tapering 3D ropes of uneven
  thickness, matched to the colour of the game's own body blood. A hit sprays a fine mist out
  of the exit side, and heavier drops fly on to splat the floor and the wall behind him.
  `vr_bloodFX 0` brings back the game's old sprites.
- **Pumping wounds:** for the first seconds a wound throws a stream into the air with the
  heartbeat (arteries most), then seeps and drips. The heart stops about 4 seconds after
  death; wounds keep dripping a while after, and the drips pool under him.
- **Running blood:** blood creeps from each wound over the skin the way gravity pulls at that
  moment, for 30 seconds, in a wandering trail. It runs down a standing man, curves round when
  he falls, stops where the skin lies flat, and stays where it ran. Wounds drip from where the
  blood has got to.
- **No more Soldier of Fortune 2 bullet holes:** nothing is painted on round a bullet hole.
  The real hole is cut into the body, and the blood soaks out round it over 15 seconds.
- **Blood stays put:** the splash and soak round a wound and the trails are fixed to their
  spot of the skin, so they don't slide about as a body bends or ragdolls.
- **Fixed:** one and the same big blot pattern showing on every bloodied body (one blood
  picture was being stretched over the whole body); straight ruled lines across bodies from
  blood trails; blood streaks bouncing around the room; blood appearing at your own gun when
  standing over a downed enemy; a red see-through shape where a limb had been shot off.

## Gore and the knife
- **Dead bodies take bullets.** Shots used to pass through a body lying where its ragdoll
  fell; now it is knocked, holed and bleeds where you hit it.
- **Knife on an enemy on his feet:** a slit is cut where the blade went in, and it bleeds.
  Nothing comes off.
- **Knife on a dead body:** a hit at the neck takes the head off in one blow (it can still be
  thrown). The living keep their heads, down or not.
- **Knife on an enemy down on the ground, or dead:** a hit on an arm or leg cuts across it,
  deeper with each blow in the same place, and the limb comes off once it is cut through.

## Physics
- Bodies have a real man's weight distribution (heavier head, lighter forearms and hands),
  and the physics steps 125 times a second instead of 62 (`vr_realWeights 0` for the earlier
  bodies).
- Ragdolls have a neck joint: the neck bends where it leaves the shoulders, so heads no longer
  swing out of their collars.
- Dead bodies stay where they fall on stairs instead of sliding down them.
- A ragdoll's parts no longer fly apart on the first body of a map, and moving doors and lifts
  no longer overshoot and snap back in the physics.
- The physics has a time budget per frame (`vr_physicsBudget 6`, milliseconds; 0 for no
  limit), so a pile of moving bodies slows the physics for a moment instead of the frame rate.

## Performance
No change to what you see.
- A bullet hitting a body used to hold the game up 15 to 35 ms while its wounds were worked out
  again, more the more wounds he had. Now about 2.5 ms: only what the new hole can reach is
  redone, with exactly the same result. A limb coming off went from about 100 ms to about 20.
- All of a body's blood is drawn in one pass (it was up to nine), and the renderer lays it on
  about 15 times faster. `vr_bloodAtlas 0` goes back to the old way, to compare.
- Blood in the air too thin on screen to show its shape is one flat quad instead of a 3D
  shape, so big fights no longer run the renderer out of polygons.
- Blood layers aren't drawn on far-away bodies.

## Survival
- Every body is removed 30 seconds after death, lopers included, and respawned enemies carry
  no blood, holes or wounds over from the one before.
- Waves are RealRTCW's own size again. The doubled pace of earlier builds is now
  `g_survivalRush 2` (default 1).

## Shooting
- Your bullets go exactly where the sights point, in VR and on the monitor. The random scatter
  is gone. Shotguns keep their pellet pattern; enemy accuracy is unchanged.

## Steam achievements
The mod is started by its own .bat files, not by RealRTCW's launcher, and that launcher was the
game's only line to Steam, so no achievement was ever recorded. They now go straight to Steam.
RealRTCW's own rules still apply: none with cheats on (so not on a map started with `devmap`).
Steam has to be running.

## Settings
- `vr_activeRagdolls 0/1`: the physical reactions (1).
- `vr_activeStrength 1`: muscle and balance strength (0.6 floppier, 1.4 stiffer).
- `vr_activeHits 0/1`: hit reactions on his feet (1).
- `vr_activeEuphoria 0/1`: the stagger, fall and curl on death and knockdown; 0 means plain
  limp ragdolls (1).
- `g_bleedOut 0/1`: wounds that bleed him out over time (1).
- `vr_bloodFX 0/1`: the new blood in the air (1).
- `vr_realWeights 0/1`: real body weights and 125 Hz physics (1).
- `vr_physicsBudget 6`: physics time per frame in milliseconds.
- `g_survivalRush 1`: Survival pace (2 doubles the enemies).
- `vr_log 1`: writes each shot and fall to `rtcwconsole.log` for bug reports.
